Where your skills can take you

Our graduate outcomes presentation highlights routes through applied science, ecology, research and communication. Use your coursework, fieldwork and research projects to build evidence of the skills you can offer.

Applied science

Crop protection, plant health and trials. Build evidence through a clear trial protocol, data analysis and a practical management recommendation.

Ecology & conservation

Surveying, consultancy and conservation. Develop an identification or survey record and practise writing a concise ecological report.

Research

Research assistance, technical work and doctoral study. Keep a reproducible analysis and a clear explanation of your research question and methods.

Communication

Technical writing, education and knowledge exchange. Create a technical brief or teaching resource for a defined audience.

These are examples of career directions and portfolio evidence, not a prediction of individual employment outcomes. Explore current opportunities.

How these highlights are selected and updated

Selected highlights include a short summary and a prompt for your study or research. Alongside them, automatic feeds collect article titles and publication details from entomological organisations and journals. Automated entries are discovery links, not assessments of research quality. A research label describes the source; review the article type and peer-review status at the publisher.

Subject tags may be inferred from title keywords or a journal’s scope and can be imperfect. UK focus means the item explicitly concerns the UK; a UK publisher alone does not make a story UK-focused. This is a starting point, not a comprehensive literature search.

Automatic highlights are balanced across sources and displayed by publication date, so larger feeds do not dominate. Source checks run daily. If a source is unavailable, previously collected items are retained and its status is shown below. Older selected highlights remain useful background reading.

Saved articles and your colour preference stay in this browser. There is no sign-in or cross-device sync and no hub analytics. External sites have their own privacy policies.
